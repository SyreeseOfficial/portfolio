I read this on a horror stories subreddit where people post things they say really happened. I assumed it was made up. It's a real case, and it's on Wikipedia.

## What happened

On November 5, 1975, seven loggers were driving home through the Arizona forest at dusk. In a clearing ahead, a glowing disc hovered a few feet off the ground.

Six of them wanted to leave. The seventh, 22-year-old Travis Walton, got out of the truck and walked toward it. A beam of light hit him and threw him to the ground. His crew drove off in a panic.

When they came back about half an hour later, the clearing was empty. Walton was gone.

Police suspected the crew had killed him. Five of the six men passed polygraph tests, and one was inconclusive. Five days later, Walton called his sister from a phone booth outside a gas station. He told her he'd only been gone a couple of hours. He remembered waking up on a table with large-eyed figures standing over him.

## Things that stay with me

- Walton lost about ten pounds in five days.
- Nobody in the truck has ever changed their story.
- He still says it happened. Skeptics say it was a hoax to get out of a logging contract, and nobody has proved either side.
- The movie *Fire in the Sky* (1993) is based on his book.

I think about those six men a lot. They watched a light knock their friend down and they drove away. Then they went back for him and found nothing.
