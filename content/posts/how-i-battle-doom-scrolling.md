---
title: How I Actually Battle Doom Scrolling (And Win Most Days)
date: 2026-09-09
---

I doom scroll. You probably do too.

I haven't got this figured out. I still slip sometimes and end up scrolling through stuff I swore I was done with. But I've gotten better, and a few things have worked.

## The Nuclear Options

- Delete social media entirely. Not deactivated, deleted. If I want to check something, I have to open a browser, which is annoying enough that I usually don't bother.
- Lock distracting apps behind screen time codes so I can't open them without entering one. It's just hard enough that I stop and ask whether I actually want to use the app.
- Block YouTube Shorts with an extension called [Unhook](https://unhook.app/) on Firefox. I run it on my phone, tablet, and laptop so there's nowhere to fall back to.

## The Boring Phone Strategy

I deleted all the music from my phone and only listen to podcasts now. It sucks at the gym. Trying to hit a PR while Joe Rogan talks about aliens isn't ideal. But I want my phone to be useful and nothing else.

- Black background, and grayscale mode some of the time, so there's nothing visually interesting about opening it.
- If I haven't used an app in two or three weeks, I delete it. If I haven't touched it that long, I don't need it sitting there tempting me.

## The Bedroom Setup

I bought an actual alarm clock, and my phone stays in the living room, so I can't doom scroll in bed.

Is this extreme? Yeah. But being able to focus is worth it. I'd rather have my attention than another app I'll forget about in a week.
