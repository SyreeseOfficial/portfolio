I've been meaning to start a blog for a long time. Here it is.

No editorial calendar. No newsletter. No niche. I'm going to write when I have something to say — some days that'll be about building software, some days it'll just be something that's been sitting in my head.

## Why

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain something in plain text, I don't actually understand it yet.

Also, I build things daily and I've started losing track of what I learned. A log of some kind felt overdue.

## What To Expect

Probably some mix of:

- Things I'm building and why
- Technical problems I actually solved
- Honest observations about being an indie builder
- Random thoughts that don't fit anywhere else

No agenda. Just honest writing.
