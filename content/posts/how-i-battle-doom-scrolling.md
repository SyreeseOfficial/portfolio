---
title: How I Actually Battle Doom Scrolling (And Win Most Days)
date: 2026-09-09
---

Doom scrolling sucks. I do it. You do it. We all do it.

I'm not gonna sit here and pretend I've got this figured out completely. I still slip sometimes. Still find myself scrolling through stuff I swore I was done with. But I've gotten better, and honestly, I've found some stuff that actually works.

## The Nuclear Options

- Delete social media entirely. Not deactivated. Deleted. If I want to check something, I have to go to a browser, which is annoying enough that I usually don't bother.
- Lock distracting apps behind screen time locks so I physically can't open them without entering a code. Makes it just hard enough that I stop and think about whether I actually want to use it.
- Block YouTube Shorts with an extension called [Unhook](https://unhook.app/) on Firefox. I use it on my phone, tablet, and laptop so there's nowhere to fall back to.

## The Boring Phone Strategy

Here's where it gets weird: I deleted all music from my phone. Just gone. Now I only listen to podcasts. Yeah, it sucks at the gym. Trying to crush a PR while Joe Rogan's talking about aliens isn't ideal. But the point is to make my phone boring. Make it so the only thing it does is be useful, not entertaining.

- Black background. Grayscale mode on sometimes. Nothing visually interesting about opening it.
- Delete unused apps. If an app hasn't been used in two or three weeks, it's gone. If I haven't touched it in that long, I clearly don't need it sitting there tempting me.

## The Setup That Actually Changed Things

Bought an actual alarm clock for my bedroom. My phone stays in the living room. Can't doom scroll in bed if your phone isn't there.

Are these extreme? Yeah. But the ability to focus is worth it. I'd rather have my attention than another app I'll forget about in a week.
