---
title: How My Sister Tricked Me Into Running 13 Miles (And I'm Doing It in 3 Days)
date: 2026-09-16
---

I hate running.

I'm 27, turning 28. Last time I actually ran was high school when I was on the basketball team. Nothing since then. So when my sister flew in for my nephew's graduation and somehow I ended up signed up for a 13-mile obstacle course race, that definitely wasn't the plan.

## How This Happened

My sister and I were just hanging out talking, and YouTube came up. You know those endurance running videos? All muddy people looking heroic with some sick soundtrack. Looks cool until you remember you hate running.

She asked if I'd ever done anything like that. I hadn't. Farthest I'd gone was seven miles, and that was just me being random one day, running laps around a track to see if I was still in shape. Years ago. Turns out I wasn't.

Then she's like, "There's a Spartan Race in Seattle. We should do it."

I didn't know what a Spartan Race actually was. I thought it was just some obstacles and some mud or something. The sound of it kind of made me nervous though, to be honest.

Then I completely forgot about it for like three months.

## The Plot Twist

Fast forward a few months. Something reminds me the race exists and I text my sister about it. She tells me she can't go, her husband's got something that weekend. He's a retired Green Beret. But I was already committed. So I jumped on the Spartan website and signed up before I could talk myself out of it.

So now I'm doing this alone. Still haven't trained. But backing out felt worse than just showing up and getting destroyed. Time to start my training.

## I Did Everything Wrong

So now it's September. The race is in three days. And yeah, I went way too hard with my training.

I bought all the gear, Strava app, running shoes, socks, a watch, all of it. Wanted to feel like an actual runner. And for a few weeks I was crushing it. Felt strong, felt excited about something I'd basically signed up as a joke.

Then I did something stupid.

My buddy and I were gonna do a four-mile run. But I'd already done two miles before he showed up. So we ended up doing 13 miles, his first half marathon, my second. Fifteen and a half miles in one day as a BEGINNER runner.

- Next day: ran five more miles even though I was hurting
- Day after that: ran two more miles despite my left ankle feeling like it was gonna snap
- That was two weeks ago. My ankle and calf still hurt like hell.

## The Point of No Return

I'm pretty sure I gave myself a minor stress fracture. Haven't run in two weeks. The pain's still there like a dull ache that gets worse when I actually test it. My brain's telling me to just stay home, ice it, don't be an idiot.

But the race is in three days.

So I'm just gonna send it.

I have no idea if I'll finish. Might be limping through mud at mile six cursing my sister. But I paid for it, I trained for it (badly), and I'm scared of it. That's apparently the thing that makes you actually show up.
