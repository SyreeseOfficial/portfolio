---
title: How My Sister Tricked Me Into Running 13 Miles (And I'm Doing It in 3 Days)
date: 2026-09-16
---

I hate running.

I'm 27, turning 28, and the last time I ran was in high school on the basketball team. So when my sister flew in for my nephew's graduation and I somehow ended up signed up for a 13-mile obstacle course race, that was not the plan.

## How This Happened

My sister and I were hanging out and YouTube came up. You know those endurance running videos, with muddy people looking heroic over a sick soundtrack? They look cool until you remember you hate running.

She asked if I'd ever done anything like that. I hadn't. My farthest was seven miles, one random day years ago when I ran laps around a track to see if I was still in shape. I wasn't.

Then she said, "There's a Spartan Race in Seattle. We should do it."

I didn't know what a Spartan Race was. I figured it was some obstacles and mud. The name made me a little nervous, though. Then I forgot about it for three months.

## Signing Up Alone

A few months later something reminded me the race existed, so I texted my sister. She told me she couldn't go because her husband, a retired Green Beret, had something that weekend. I'd already told myself I was doing it, so I jumped on the Spartan website and signed up before I could talk myself out of it.

That left me doing it alone, with no training. Backing out felt worse than showing up and getting destroyed, so I started training.

## Training Too Hard

It's September now, the race is in three days, and I went way too hard.

I bought everything: the Strava app, running shoes, socks, a watch. I wanted to feel like a runner, and for a few weeks it worked. I felt strong and excited about something I'd signed up for as a joke.

Then I did something stupid. My buddy and I planned a four-mile run, but I'd already done two miles before he showed up, and we ended up running 13. His first half marathon, my second. That's 15 and a half miles in one day as a beginner.

- Next day: five more miles, even though I was hurting
- Day after: two more miles, with my left ankle feeling like it might snap

That was two weeks ago, and my ankle and calf still hurt like hell.

## Three Days Out

I'm pretty sure I gave myself a minor stress fracture. I haven't run in two weeks. There's a dull ache that gets worse when I test it, and my brain keeps telling me to stay home and ice it.

But I paid for the race, so I'm going to send it.

I don't know if I'll finish. I might be limping through mud at mile six, cursing my sister. I trained for it, badly, and I'm scared of it, and that seems to be what gets you to show up.
