I've trained two days a week for 15 years. That's it.

Sounds lazy, but it isn't. I learned it from Alex Leonidas, an old school fitness YouTuber who's been around forever. I watched him back in the day, bought his program Naturally Enhanced, and it clicked. He taught people to train twice a week and spend two other days on GPP (General Physical Preparedness). I've basically run that ever since.

I've taken breaks and tried other programs here and there, some random stuff for a few months. I always come back to this one because it works. It's simple, but it's not easy. You still put in serious work.

## The Split

- **Heavy day:** sets of 5 to 10 reps, going for PRs
- **Volume day:** sets of 10 to 25 reps, sometimes 100 to 200 if you just want to pump blood into the muscles for recovery

## Switch Exercises Every Week

Don't hammer the same movement every time. Your joints and connective tissue get a break while your muscles keep working. A chest rotation might look like this:

- Week 1: bench press
- Week 2: dumbbell press

You hit chest every time, just from a different angle. You recover better because the same exercise isn't beating you up week after week.

## Why It Works for Natural Lifters

I'm a natural lifter, no drugs, no PEDs. Naturals recover way slower than someone juicing, and you need about one to two days after a hard workout. Two sessions a week fits that.

You walk into the gym fresh instead of beat up from training three days ago, so you can give full effort. I train to failure on pretty much every set because I have the recovery to back it up. Train more days a week and you'd run yourself into the ground.

## Bottom Line

Train heavy one day, train volume one day, vary your exercises, and recover between sessions.
